# Jensen's Inequality
For a [[Optimization#^bec74f|convex function]] $f$ and a [[Random Variables|random variable]] $X$:

$$
f(\mathbb E[X]) \le \mathbb E[f(X)]
$$

Similarly, for a concave function $g$ and a random variable $Y$, the inequality says:

$$
g(\mathbb E[Y]) \ge \mathbb E[g(Y)]
$$
